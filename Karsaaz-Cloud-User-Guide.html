👋 Welcome to Karsaaz Cloud

Karsaaz Cloud is your private, secure workspace for storing files, sharing them with your team, and getting work done — from any device.

Think of it as your own private Dropbox + Google Drive + Slack rolled into one — except your data never leaves Karsaaz servers. No third party sees your files. No advertising. No tracking.

How to access Karsaaz Cloud

Where How
🌐 Web browser Open any browser and visit your Karsaaz Cloud address. Log in with your username and password.
📱 Phone / tablet Install the Nextcloud app from Apple App Store or Google Play (Karsaaz Cloud uses the same app). Enter your Karsaaz Cloud address when asked.
💻 Computer (sync) Install the Nextcloud Desktop app for Windows, Mac, or Linux. Your files will appear like a normal folder on your computer and stay synced.
📁 Network drive Mount Karsaaz Cloud as a Windows network drive (Z:) or Mac Finder location using WebDAV — no separate app needed.

How you'll get your account

  1. Your Karsaaz Cloud admin creates the account for you — you can't sign up on your own (this keeps the workspace private).
  2. You'll get a welcome email from Karsaaz Cloud with a link to set your password.
  3. Click the link, choose a strong password (at least 10 characters, not from any breach database — Karsaaz Cloud checks this automatically).
  4. You'll be prompted to set up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on first login — required for admins, recommended for everyone.
  5. Save your 10 backup codes in case you ever lose your phone — print them or store in a password manager.
  6. Done — you're in. The Dashboard is your home page.
💡 First time logging in? Have your phone ready with Google Authenticator, Authy, or Microsoft Authenticator already installed. You'll scan a QR code to set up 2FA in 60 seconds.

🛡️ Your security

Karsaaz Cloud takes security seriously. Here's what protects your data:

🔑
Two-Factor Authentication (REQUIRED for admins)
After your password, enter a 6-digit code from an authenticator app. Mandatory for all admin accounts. Strongly recommended for everyone — even if someone steals your password, they can't get in without your phone.
📋
Backup codes
Lost your phone? Use one of the 10 printable backup codes you saved when you set up 2FA.
🚨
Suspicious-login alerts
If someone logs into your account from a strange country or new device, you get an email immediately.
🛡️
Brute-force protection
Anyone trying to guess your password gets locked out automatically after a few wrong tries.
🔐
Strong passwords required
Karsaaz Cloud rejects weak passwords and ones leaked in past data breaches. Your password must be at least 10 characters.
🙈
Privacy controls
Choose what shows on your profile — hide your email, phone, or avatar from teammates if you prefer.
💡 Pro tip: Set up 2FA the very first time you log in. It takes 60 seconds and is the single biggest thing you can do to protect your account.

🆘 Need help?

If you run into trouble, here's what to do:

  1. Check the in-app help. Click the ? icon in the top right.
  2. Talk to your Karsaaz Cloud admin. They can reset passwords, restore deleted files outside the 30-day window, or grant you access to shared folders.
  3. Visit our website at karsaaz.com for guides, tutorials, and contact info.
  4. For urgent issues (account locked, suspicious activity), contact your admin immediately.
